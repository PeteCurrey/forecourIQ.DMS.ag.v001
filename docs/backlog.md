# ForecourIQ DMS — Post-v1 Product Backlog

---

## 1. Version 1.1 (Near-Term Enhancements)
- **Personalized Customer Video Presentations**: Built-in screen and camera recorder for sales staff to generate bespoke vehicle walkaround links.
- **Intelligent Customer-to-Group-Stock Matcher**: Automated buyer alert matching customer search criteria across all group forecourt sites.
- **Advanced Bulk Price Update Staging**: Multi-vehicle price adjustment tool with margin simulation preview before bulk portal dispatch.

## 2. Version 1.2 (Mid-Term Evolution)
- **Deeper Multi-Site Financial Consolidated Reporting**: Group-level P&L rollups and inter-branch invoicing automation.
- **Automated WhatsApp Business Messaging**: Direct WhatsApp template messaging for lead follow-ups via Twilio/Meta Cloud API.
- **Enhanced Digital Signature Biometrics**: SMS OTP signing verification for remote finance order forms.

## 3. Explicitly Deferred / Post-v1 Expansion
- **Heavy Workshop Management & Job Cards**: Deferred to maintain sharp focus on independent vehicle retail operations.
- **Parts Inventory & Microfiche Cataloging**: Deferred to prevent legacy DMS bloat.
- **OEM Franchise Systems (DMS integrations for manufacturer warranty)**: Out of initial independent dealer scope.
