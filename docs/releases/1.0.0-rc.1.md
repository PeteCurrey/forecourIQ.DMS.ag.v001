## 1. Summary of Capabilities

ForecourIQ DMS v1.0 Release Candidate establishes an end-to-end operational operating system for UK automotive dealerships:

### Operational Core
- **Stockbook & Inventory**: Full vehicle lifecycle tracking, CAP/DVLA lookup integration, acquisition costing, prep board workflow, and multi-photo media management.
- **CRM & Unified Communications**: Omnichannel enquiry ingestion (AutoTrader, website, walk-in), customer interaction ledger, automated SLA tracking, test drive & appointment scheduling.
- **Deal Desk & Digital Retailing**: Multi-part order builder, live Part-Exchange valuation & equity balance, deposit receipting, finance proposal staging, and compliant vehicle handover.
- **Compliance & Document Control**: FCA Initial Disclosure Documents (IDD), Demands & Needs statements, Commission Disclosure records, and tamper-evident audit trails.
- **Multi-Site Stock Control & Transfers**: Group-wide stock visibility, formal transfer state machine (`requested` $\rightarrow$ `approved` $\rightarrow$ `scheduled` $\rightarrow$ `in_transit` $\rightarrow$ `received`), and permanent `vehicle_location_history` ledger.
- **Internal Dealership Team Chat**: Tenant-isolated messaging with dedicated channels (`#general`, `#sales`, `#prep`), direct messages, entity threads linked to vehicles/deals, Supabase Realtime sync, and `@mentions`.

### Intelligence & Management
- **IQ Operating Layer**: Daily morning briefings, proactive inventory recommendations, controlled action proposals with human-in-the-loop approval, and emergency circuit breakers.
- **Role-Based Home Views**: Tailored dashboard experiences for Dealer Principals, Sales Managers, Sales Executives, Buyers, and Compliance staff with automatic gross margin redaction where unauthorized.
- **Operational Instrumentation**: Restrained linear gauges with deterministic denominators for Stock Freshness, Lead SLA Response, and Advertising Readiness.

## 3. Pilot-Specific Constraints
1. **Scope Freeze**: Major product scope is frozen. New module domains (workshop, parts catalog, payroll) are deferred to post-v1.
2. **Pilot Owner Assignment**: Every pilot dealership is assigned a dedicated ForecourIQ platform operator with active health monitoring.
3. **Data Isolation**: Sample and demonstration datasets are isolated from real commercial pilot records.
